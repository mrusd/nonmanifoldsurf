Midsurface thickening: slab-and-union

How thicken_midsurface.py turns a (non-manifold) planar midsurface into a solid, offsetting each face by ±t along its normal (total thickness 2t).

flowchart TD
    A(["Input STEP midsurface
+ offset t per side"]) --> B["Parse STEP
faces, loops, vertices"]
    B --> C["Prepare each face
merge collinear points · unit normal n
local frame u,v · loop CCW about n"]
    C --> D{{"For every edge E of every face A"}}

    subgraph JUNC ["1 · Junction detection (geometric, ignores STEP topology)"]
      D --> E["Compare E with every other face B"]
      E --> F{"E lies in
B's plane?"}
      F -- no --> E
      F -- yes --> G{"E overlaps one of
B's boundary edges?"}
      G -- yes --> H["Record branch direction b
(B's inward in-plane direction)"]
      G -- no --> I{"Midpoint of E strictly
inside B?"}
      I -- yes --> J["Mark: lands on B's interior"]
      I -- no --> E
    end

    H --> K
    J --> K
    subgraph CLS ["2 · Classify edge E of face A  (a = A's inward direction)"]
      K{"Lands on a
face interior?"}
      K -- yes --> BR1["BRANCH
extend by t"]
      K -- no --> L{"Some b = −a?
(A continues coplanar)"}
      L -- yes --> TH["THROUGH
extend by ε (tiny overlap)"]
      L -- no --> M{"Number of distinct
branch directions"}
      M -- "1" --> MI["MITER
φ = angle(a, b)
extend t/tan(φ/2) + t
clip plane ⟂ bisector of a, b"]
      M -- "≥ 2" --> BR2["BRANCH
extend by t (T / Y junction)"]
      M -- "0" --> FR["FREE
no extension, flat cap"]
    end

    BR1 --> N
    TH --> N
    MI --> N
    BR2 --> N
    FR --> N

    subgraph SLAB ["3 · Build one slab per face"]
      N["Extended polygon:
offset each edge line outward by its extension,
intersect consecutive lines"]
      N --> O["Sweep polygon from −t to +t along n
(prism)"]
      O --> P["Trim by each miter half-space
keep m·(x − p₀) ≥ 0"]
    end

    P --> Q{"Backend"}
    subgraph UNION ["4 · Union and clean-up"]
      Q -- "OpenCASCADE (exact B-rep)" --> R["Multi-argument Boolean fuse
(fuzzy tolerance)"]
      R --> S["Unify same-domain faces
(merge coplanar faces)"]
      Q -- "manifold3d (mesh)" --> T["Batch mesh union"]
    end

    S --> U["Validate
BRepCheck · solid count · volume"]
    T --> U
    U --> V(["Outputs
_thick.step · _thick.stl · _thick.html"])

Edge kinds on the rib-box model (t = 20 mm)

KindRuleWhere it occurs here
FREENo other face touches the edge; slab ends flush. Wall tops (z = 300), rib top and rib free end (y = 300)
THROUGHFace continues in the same plane; only a tiny overlap ε for a clean fuse. The two front-wall halves at x = 500
MITEROne neighbour at angle φ: extend, then cut on the bisector. Both neighbours use the same plane, so they meet exactly. Floor–wall edges and wall–wall corners (φ = 90°, extension 40 mm before trimming)
BRANCHEdge lands on a face's interior, or meets 2+ non-coplanar branches; extend by t to penetrate. Rib bottom into the floor, rib side into the front wall (both extended 20 mm)

Why geometric junction detection

  • The STEP export duplicated the rib–wall edge and never imprinted the rib onto the floor.
  • Testing "edge lies in plane + overlaps boundary / crosses interior" finds both joins anyway.
  • Tolerance defaults to 1e-5 × model size (0.01 mm here), matching the STEP's accuracy.

Checks on the result

  • One valid solid, 15 faces after unification.
  • Volume 91,200,000 mm³ = 1040²·320 − 960²·280 + 40·280·280.
  • Bounding box (−20, −20, −20) to (1020, 1020, 300).
